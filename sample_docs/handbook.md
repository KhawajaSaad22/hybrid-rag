# Example company handbook — fictional demo data

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Managers approve the schedule.
Work from another country requires prior approval from the People team.

## Vacation
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual vacation. Submit vacation requests
at least ten business days in advance. Managers approve requests in the HR portal.

## Security
Report a lost laptop immediately to the security team. The security team revokes
active sessions and remotely locks the device. Never share passwords in support tickets.
